# Heaven's Calendar Support

Welcome to the support page for **Heaven's Calendar**. 

Heaven's Calendar is a client-side, offline-first mobile application designed to align your schedule with the 364-day Enoch celestial cycle, astronomical dawn markers, and biblical feast days.

---

### Frequently Asked Questions

**1. Why does the app request Location permissions?**  
Heaven's Calendar uses on-device astronomical algorithms (`SunCalc`) to calculate the exact timing of dawn, sunrise, and solar altitude for your exact coordinate. Your GPS data never leaves your device.

**2. Why didn't I receive the Shofar alert?**  
Ensure that notification permissions are enabled in your device's system settings under **Heaven's Calendar > Notifications**, and that Do Not Disturb or Focus modes allow scheduled calendar alerts.

**3. Does the app work offline?**  
Yes. The entire 50-year Enoch calendar dataset, lunar calculations, and guidelines run 100% on-device without an internet connection.
