# Privacy Policy for Heaven's Calendar

**Last updated:** September 18, 2026

Heaven's Calendar ("we," "our," or "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information. 

Heaven's Calendar is designed as an offline-first, client-side utility. We do not require account registration, and we do not collect, sell, or monetize your personal information.

---

## 1. Information We Access and How We Use It

### Location Data (Foreground Only)
* **Purpose:** The application requests access to your device's approximate or precise location solely to calculate accurate astronomical solar coordinates (such as astronomical dawn, sunrise, and sunset) using local mathematical algorithms.
* **On-Device Processing Only:** All solar and celestial computations occur entirely on your device. Your location coordinates (latitude and longitude) are never transmitted to external servers, stored in remote databases, shared with third parties, or used for tracking purposes.
* **Revocable Permission:** You can disable location permissions at any time through your device's system settings. If disabled, the application defaults to predetermined reference coordinates.

### Local Storage & Device Preferences
* The application utilizes local device storage (SQLite and native storage) to cache calendar configurations, user preferences, and notification schedules.
* This data remains strictly on your device and is never synchronized with an external server.

---

## 2. Notifications

* Heaven's Calendar uses local device notifications to deliver alerts for holy days, Sabbaths, and morning dawn markers.
* These notifications are scheduled and triggered directly by your device's operating system. No remote push notification servers, device tokens, or external messaging services (such as APNs or FCM push servers) are used to track or message your device.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Analytics

* **No Advertising:** The application contains no third-party advertisements or ad-tracking SDKs.
* **No Analytics Tracking:** We do not collect behavioral analytics, device identifiers (IDFA/GAID), or usage tracking telemetry.
* **Weather Data (Optional):** If weather data integration is active, weather queries request standard atmospheric conditions using your coordinates without sending personal identifiers.

---

## 4. Children’s Privacy

Heaven's Calendar does not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13. The application is safe for all audiences and contains no data collection mechanisms.
